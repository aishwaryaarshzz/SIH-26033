## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FarmConnect is a web-based platform that connects farmers directly with consumers and retailers, reducing unnecessary intermediaries in the agricultural supply chain.

1. **Detailed explanation of the proposed solution**
   - Farmers can register and create profiles on the platform.
   - Farmers can upload agricultural products, quantities, prices, and product images.
   - Consumers can browse products, compare prices, and place orders directly with farmers.
   - The platform provides order management and delivery coordination.

2. **How it addresses the problem**
   - Reduces dependence on multiple intermediaries.
   - Helps farmers receive a better share of the selling price.
   - Provides consumers with fresh products at transparent prices.
   - Improves communication between farmers and buyers.

3. **Innovation and uniqueness of the solution**
   - Direct farmer-to-consumer online marketplace.
   - Transparent product pricing and availability.
   - Location-based product discovery.
   - Digital order tracking and farmer ratings.
   - A simple, user-friendly interface for rural farmers.

## Technical Approach
1. **Technologies to be used**
   - Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap.
   - Backend: Python and Django framework.
   - Database: SQLite for the prototype.
   - Version control: Git and GitHub.
   - Development environment: Visual Studio Code.

2. **Methodology and implementation process**
   - Design the user interface for farmers and consumers.
   - Develop farmer and consumer registration and login.
   - Create database models for users, products, orders, and payments.
   - Implement product listing, searching, price comparison, and ordering.
   - Add order status updates and delivery coordination.
   - Test the application and fix errors.
   - Deploy the application after successful testing.

3. **Technical approach diagram**

   Farmer → FarmConnect Web Interface → Django Backend → Database → Consumer

   Farmers upload products and prices. The backend processes requests and stores information in the database. Consumers browse products and place orders through the web interface.
## Feasibility and Viability
1. **Feasibility of the idea**
   - The project can be developed using free and open-source technologies.
   - Django provides built-in authentication and database integration.
   - The prototype can run on a normal computer with an internet connection.
   - The website can be accessed through desktop and mobile browsers.

2. **Potential challenges and risks**
   - Limited digital knowledge among some farmers.
   - Internet connectivity problems in rural areas.
   - Delivery and transportation costs.
   - Fake product listings and fraudulent orders.
   - Payment security and protection of user information.

3. **Strategies for overcoming challenges**
   - Provide a simple interface and clear instructions.
   - Design responsive pages for mobile devices.
   - Support pickup and local delivery options.
   - Verify farmer accounts and allow customers to report issues.
   - Use secure authentication and trusted payment gateways.
## Impact and Benefits
1. **Potential impact on the target audience**
   - Farmers can reach more customers without depending entirely on intermediaries.
   - Consumers can discover agricultural products and compare prices.
   - Small retailers can connect with local farmers.
   - Rural communities can benefit from improved digital market access.

2. **Benefits of the solution**
   - Economic: Potentially improves farmer earnings and reduces unnecessary margins.
   - Social: Builds direct relationships between farmers and consumers.
   - Environmental: Local sourcing may reduce transportation distances and food waste.
   - Technological: Encourages digital adoption in agriculture.
   - Transparency: Displays product prices and availability clearly.

3. **Expected outcome**
   - A functional prototype demonstrating direct product listing, searching, and ordering.
   - A more transparent agricultural marketplace.
   - Reduced dependence on unnecessary intermediaries where direct selling is practical.
